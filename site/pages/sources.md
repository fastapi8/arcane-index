## Direct in-game research

Most cooking calculations, boundary behavior, recipe energy values, and regression cases documented by Arcane Index were tested directly in Arcane Odyssey by the project contributors.

See [Contributors](/contributors) for research credits.

## Arcane Odyssey Wiki

The Arcane Odyssey Wiki was used as a reference for ingredient information, modifier mechanics, fish mechanics, developer confirmations attributed to Vetex, and other documented game behavior.

Content from the wiki is used with attribution under its CC BY-SA license.

- Arcane Odyssey Wiki — [link](https://roblox-arcane-odyssey.fandom.com/wiki/Arcane_Odyssey_Wiki)
- License: CC BY-SA — [link](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)

Individual mechanics pages should cite the relevant wiki page where appropriate.

## Arcane Odyssey Cookbook

The community Arcane Odyssey Cookbook spreadsheet was used as a reference for
known cooking values for fish.

All other ingredients were either experimentally derived or tested in game by
our contributors.

- Arcane Odyssey Cookbook — [Google Sheets link](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1PqHB6xkWHoaRLWmnZnJYiGK4XoS4VBYQRt4y00A1HVw/edit?gid=14377493#gid=14377493)
